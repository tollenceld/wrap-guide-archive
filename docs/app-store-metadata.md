# App Store Metadata Draft

## English

- **Name:** Wrap Guide
- **Subtitle:** Measure paper. Follow each fold.
- **Primary category:** Utilities
- **Age rating:** Expected 4+, subject to the current App Store questionnaire
- **Description:**

  Wrap Guide helps you wrap a rectangular gift box without guessing how much paper to cut or which fold comes next. Scan a box or enter its measurements, choose a paper plan, then follow clear spatial construction lines one step at a time.

  Easy Wrap adds beginner-friendly margin. Just Fit saves paper. If you already have a sheet, Wrap Guide checks its size, rotation, and diagonal placement locally on your iPhone.

  No account. No uploaded camera video. No ads or tracking.

- **Keywords:** gift wrap,wrapping paper,box measurement,AR guide,present,folding
- **Review note:** Place a regular phone or shoe box on a well-lit table and tap “Scan a gift box.” A complete manual path is available from the first screen. Simulator builds use an explicitly labeled fixture; physical devices use the camera. No login or external hardware is required.

## 简体中文

- **名称：** Wrap Guide
- **副标题：** 算好纸张，看清每一折
- **描述：**

  Wrap Guide 帮你包装规则长方体礼盒，不再猜测应该裁多大的纸，也不必反复暂停视频寻找下一步。

  扫描礼盒或手动输入尺寸，选择省纸的 Just Fit 或更适合新手的 Easy Wrap，然后根据现实画面中的折线、方向和胶带位置逐步完成包装。已有包装纸时，App 还会在本地判断尺寸、旋转和对角摆放是否可行。

  无需账号，不上传相机画面，没有广告或跟踪。

- **关键词：** 礼物包装,包装纸,礼盒测量,AR 指导,折纸,礼物
