# Wrap Guide privacy notice

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Wrap Guide performs wrapping-paper calculations locally on the user's device. It does not require an account or network connection and does not request access to the camera, microphone, photos, location, contacts, Bluetooth, or tracking permission.

Wrap Guide does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It includes no advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party SDKs. The only saved preference is the selected display-unit setting in local app storage. Removing the app removes that preference.

This repository contains an internal minimum viable version, not a published App Store product. Before any public distribution, the release owner must add the legal developer identity, monitored privacy contact, applicable jurisdictional disclosures, and a public HTTPS policy URL.
