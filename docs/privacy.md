# Wrap Guide Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Wrap Guide processes camera frames, device motion, and spatial information only on the user's iPhone to measure a gift box and display wrapping guidance. Camera frames, depth information, and AR world maps are not saved or transmitted.

Wrap Guide does not:

- create user accounts;
- collect personal information;
- upload camera or spatial data;
- include advertising or third-party analytics;
- track users across apps or websites;
- request microphone, photo library, contacts, or location access.

The active wrapping session stores only box dimensions, paper dimensions, the selected plan, phone mode, and the current wrapping step on the device. The record is replaced when the user begins another wrap and is removed when the app is deleted.

Camera access is optional. Users who decline it can enter dimensions manually and use diagram-based guidance.
